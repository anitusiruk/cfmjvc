# The fifty

Fifty features were selected from the brief, chosen for depth of mechanic and for how much they feed
each other. Ideas that were mostly cosmetic, or that duplicated a deeper idea already on the list,
were dropped in their favour — those are listed at the bottom with the reason.

Decorative blocks are not counted in the fifty. They are all registered regardless, as asked.

---

## Core substance (1–5)

**1. Qi.** An amount plus a `PhaseBlend` describing what that Qi *is like*. There is no second
resource bar per element: a body of Qi has a character, and mixing incompatible characters is what
creates turbulence. Turbulence leaks, sparks, hurts alchemy, and is what Lingzhi feeds on. Stored Qi
is shown as drifting motes coloured by its blend, so you read a device by looking at it.

**2. Echo Essence.** Cannot power anything. It teaches, restores and wakes: it is what wakes an
ancestral tablet, what turns plain ink into echo ink, and what goes into a scroll or a stabilising
pill. Memory made into an item.

**3. Spirit Stones.** Raw stone refines into Low, Middle and High. Placed as blocks they are real
reservoirs, and their charge lives in the blockstate driving the light level — a wall of spirit stone
visibly dims as the workshop drinks from it.

**4. Five-Phase Essences.** Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water as qualities of Qi rather than five
separate pools. Phases relate through the generating cycle (cheap, calm) and the overcoming cycle
(expensive, turbulent). Most recipes ask for a *relationship* between phases, not a quantity.

**5. Spiritual Root Attunement.** Chosen in an attunement circuit by offering an essence, and
changeable later. One phase is clean and reliable. Two that feed each other are the strongest
ordinary choice. Two that fight each other give more force and much more risk — a deliberate gamble
rather than a bad roll at spawn.

## The cultivator (6–12)

**6. Meridian Cultivation.** Five channels — hand, foot, heart, crown, dantian — each opened by
practising the thing it governs. Fighting with a bound sword opens the hand; cloudstepping and
footwork seals open the foot; taking hits opens the heart; reading echoes opens the crown. An open
meridian is a circuit that Qi is allowed to flow through, not a stat bonus.

**7. Cultivation Realms.** Breath Gathering → Foundation → Golden Core → Nascent Spirit. Each rung
changes capability: Foundation makes formations obey you, Golden Core makes personal Qi persistent
and artifacts passive, Nascent Spirit lets you leave your body.

**8. Breakthrough Ritual.** Performed in the world. Sit still inside a running cultivation circuit
while it is fed; every second the ritual reads the phase balance, the harmony and your composure.
Good readings build stability, bad ones eat it, and at zero the attempt collapses with backlash.
Automation can do all the feeding — it cannot do the sitting.

**9. Heavenly Tribulation.** Bolts pick the tallest tagged conductor within range, then any device
holding Qi, and only then you. Each bolt that lands on a device dumps a huge, metallic, turbulent
charge into it. Put rods and reservoirs around your formation and the sky becomes a power source;
put nothing up and it becomes a problem.

**10. Golden Core.** Personal Qi with real capacity and regeneration. Dying does not destroy it, it
shakes it loose: reduced capacity and slower recovery until it settles, which is a recovery mechanic
rather than a punishment.

**11. Heart Demon.** A shadow wearing your own strength, raised partway through a dangerous
breakthrough. Hitting it *feeds* it. What erodes it is composure — a clear heart, calming incense, a
talisman stamped to quiet things. Let it land enough blows and the breakthrough fails.

**12. Nascent Spirit Projection.** Your body stays sitting where you left it as a real entity on a
leash. The spirit flies, inspects and touches seals, but cannot mine, carry or fight. Stray too far,
run out of Qi, or let the body be disturbed, and you snap back.

## The world (13–17)

**13. Dragon Veins.** Not structures and nothing is generated. Ridge fields evaluated from the world
seed on demand, so every block already knows whether a current runs through it, which way it flows,
and what phase it carries. Two crossing veins make the best cultivation site in a world.

**14. Spirit Spring Water.** A real fluid with a bucket. Plants beside it, alchemy made with it and
cultivation in it all benefit; there is no way to mass produce it, so moving it matters.

**15. Spirit Bamboo.** Grows only on ambient Qi from veins, formations or leaking devices. Feed it
too much and it shoots up hollow and brittle — which is exactly what cheap flues are made of, so the
"failure" is a resource.

**16. Moon Lotus.** Opens only under an open night sky, gathers Qi from moonlight, and sets petals
slowly. Automatable, but daylight closes it no matter what you do, which makes it a storage and
timing problem.

**17. Earthroot Ginseng.** Matures on the *variety* of blocks around it rather than on farmland, so a
monoculture field grows nothing and a deliberately mixed garden grows the good stuff.

## Qi network (18–26)

**18. Jade Bi Reservoir.** Capacity rises with how many sibling discs are nearby, tapering off, so a
ring of six holds far more than the same jade in a solid cube. The tidy layout *is* the upgrade.

**19. Bamboo Qi Flue.** Deliberately imperfect plumbing. Every section wastes a little; a corner
wastes much more; climbing wastes more still; a long unsupported run sags. Running along a dragon
vein claws some of it back.

**20. Jade Flue Joint.** Forgives one corner completely and resets the run length. The reason jade is
worth the cost.

**21. Hollow Bamboo Flue.** The cheap, lossy variant made from overfed bamboo.

**22. Jade Meridian Thread.** Carries discrete identified pulses, one block per tick, with a fixed
toll per block — so a long thread is a delay line and a branching thread is a fan-out. Wire for
signals; the bagua is for dividing power.

**23. Bagua Distributor.** Divides what arrives among the eight positions around it, corners
included. Six rules — alternate, clockwise, opposed, priority, spread, and generative, which reads
what each neighbour holds and only sends Qi where it will feed rather than fight. Click to change the
rule, sneak-click to turn the disc.

**24. Five-Phase Conversion Wheel.** One step along the generating cycle at 70%. Reversing it against
the cycle is possible, needs to have been learned, runs at 40% and roughens the whole pool.

**25. Qi Prism.** Cut facets — relay, split, focus, bend, filter, scatter — that refract an arriving
pulse into beams crossing open space. The advanced cuts have to be learned.

**26. Bronze Spirit Bell.** Rings at a pitch set by the strength of the pulse that struck it, then
passes the pulse on. Ambience, alarm, readout and instrument at once.

## Signals and logic (27–29)

**27. Resonance Stones.** Retransmit only when they hear the note they are tuned to. Tuning is done by
striking them. This is what lets a workshop be wired with sound instead of redstone under the floor.

**28. Jade Abacus.** The controller. Beads set a threshold, the frame sets what is being counted —
fullness, emptiness, pulses, tones or an interval — and it fires a pulse when the count is met. The
answer is literally the bead position.

**29. Footwork Seal.** Nearly invisible, and it reads *how* something crossed rather than that it
did. Sneaking, walking, running, leaping and cloudstepping each produce a different pulse strength
and a different tone, so wuxia movement itself becomes an input device.

## Formations (30–33)

**30. Formation Tiles.** Nodes, lines, arcs and trigram plates. Individually decorative.

**31. Formation Ink.** Drawn onto floors with a loaded brush, one stroke at a time; it cannot be
placed from the hand. Its colour is its phase, which makes a circuit readable from the doorway.

**32. Formation Circuits.** A core surveys what is drawn around it and identifies it by *shape*: a
circuit is complete when nothing in it is a dead end. Gathering, repulsion, cultivation, preservation
and attunement are recognised from parts, seals and ink phases present — never from a fixed template,
so players design their own layouts. Two things throttle a formation: the Qi in the core, and how
well what it is fed matches what it wants.

**33. Formation Banners.** Floor circuits define an area; banners give it height and direction, and
raise the strength of the effect.

## Alchemy (34–38)

**34. Ding Cauldron.** Ingredients go in by hand or are dropped in from above; heat comes from what
burns underneath and can be pushed up with Fire Qi. The state is reported by smoke, bubbling colour
and sound.

**35. Pill Quality.** The cauldron scores itself every second on heat against the recipe window,
phase match, turbulence and how dirty the vessel is, and averages the whole cook into the grade.
Nothing is rolled: Perfect pills are an engineering achievement, and residue accumulates until you
scour the pot.

**36. Alchemy Pills.** Meridian-Opening, Clear-Heart, Body-Tempering, Qi-Recovery, Cloudstep,
Five-Phase Harmony and Breakthrough-Stabilising. Every pill reads your tolerance, which rises sharply
on repeats and decays over time, so keeping one for the moment it matters beats carrying stacks.

**37. Ingredient Order.** Recipes are ordered sequences, not sets. Put something in that no recipe
starts with and the batch spoils — into salvageable residue rather than nothing.

**38. Herb Drying Rack.** Herbs hang and dry in whatever surrounds them. Sun concentrates, moonlight
refines, censer smoke infuses; the same herb becomes three different ingredients.

## Ink, seals and paper (39–42)

**39. Inkstone and Spirit Brush.** Materials are ground by hand — repeatedly — then a brush is dipped.
A half-ground well gives fewer strokes. Which material went in decides what the ink is.

**40. Seal-Carving Table.** Empty hand turns the chisel to the next rule; a blank of wood, stone,
bronze or jade becomes a seal carved with it. Softer materials wear out, jade holds an edge.

**41. Fulu / Talismans.** A talisman is a rule expressed in an ink. The rule says what happens, the
ink says in what manner — a binding seal in fire ink does not bind, it burns. Pressed onto a surface
they wait for Qi; slapped onto a creature they fire immediately, paid for out of your own Qi.

**42. Seals on Formations.** Stamping a pasted talisman changes what a circuit is being asked to be,
which is how one ring becomes a repulsion wall or a preservation field.

## Atmosphere (43)

**43. Incense Censer.** Slow, area-wide and atmospheric. Calming settles minds, revealing makes recent
echoes visible without a mirror, suppressing hurts the undead, kindling speeds cultivation, and
attuning drags every nearby device's Qi towards one phase — useful maintenance, and a nuisance if you
forget it is burning.

## The past (44–47)

**44. Echo Mirror.** The world keeps a decaying log of what happened where. The mirror reads it,
drawing ghostly repetitions at the places they happened and saying how long ago each was. This is the
mod's central verb.

**45. Ancestral Tablet.** Watches you work and writes down each interaction as a gesture — which
device, relative to itself, and what was done to it.

**46. Echo Imprinting.** When a run of gestures repeats, the tablet recognises it as a lesson. Woken
with echo essence, it replays the lesson with translucent hands, taking items from a container placed
against it. Devices opt in to being operated, so nothing pretends to be a player. Your automation
literally comes from teaching the present to imitate the past.

**47. Echo Scrolls and Discoveries.** Teachings gate *understanding*, never crafting: an unlearned
circuit will not answer, a reversed wheel will not turn. Every teaching can also be worked out by
doing the thing, so a scroll is a shortcut. A blank scroll copies something you know, so knowledge
can be handed on.

## Tools and logistics (48–50)

**48. Dragon-Vein Compass.** The inspection tool. Paints the vein under your feet and its direction,
reports what a device holds, what a circuit has been identified as, and what mark a container carries.

**49. Jade Imperial Seal.** Authorisation rather than addressing. Stamp containers with the same seal
face and they are one household. This is the whole routing system — no numbered channels.

**50. Paper Crane Familiar.** Address a crane with a seal, use it on a container, and it takes one
load and physically flies it to the nearest place stamped the same way. Slow, visible, and destroyed
by rain or a single hit. Automation that looks like magic instead of like pipes.

## Also implemented, folded into the above

- **Flying Sword** — bound blade that orbits, strikes and returns; a golden core lets it cut through
  more than one enemy.
- **Sword Intent** — the blade tallies how it has actually been used. Undead give Purifying,
  attacking from the air gives Falling Star, crowds give Flowing River, blocking gives Mountain. The
  dominant intent changes what the weapon does.
- **Sword Qi** — a charged crescent that deals modest damage but cuts vegetation, severs buffs off
  what it hits, and wakes formation lines it sweeps over.
- **Cloudstep Shoes** — midair steps that fire at the apex of a jump and again when falling fast while
  sprinting, capped so it never becomes creative flight.
- **Sword Cultivator and Alchemist sets** — piece effects plus full-set effects: the blade intercepts
  one projectile for you, robes blunt fire and magic.
- **Lingzhi Spirit Fungus** — eats turbulence out of nearby devices, turning a messy workshop into a
  crop and a tidy one into a deliberate choice.

## Cut, and why

| Idea | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Guqin, Bamboo Dizi | Note-sequence input needs client-side keybinds to feel good; the sound layer is served by bells, resonance stones and footwork seals |
| Tea Brewing Set | Overlapped pills without adding a new verb |
| Cultivator's Manual | A guidebook is mostly presentation; the discovery system carries the same progression without needing a rendered book |
| Bronze Spirit Mirror | Folded into the Echo Mirror and the compass |
| Spirit Gourd, Silk Cloud Ribbon, Jade Umbrella, Cloud-and-Moon Fan | Four separate held artifacts competing for the same slot; the sword line is the deeper one |
| Ink Servant | Duplicates the paper crane and the tablet |
| Jade Hairpin | A single emergency reserve, shallower than the golden core's instability recovery |
| Sunfire Peony, Immortal Peach | Two more generating plants on top of four already implemented |
| Spirit Kiln, Silk Loom, Moxibustion Brazier | Extra stations whose mechanics were versions of the cauldron and the censer |
| Acupuncture Needle | The meridian-sealing mechanic it wanted is implemented in `Cultivator.sealMeridian`; the item to trigger it is a small addition on top |
| Spirit Lantern | Talisman-modified light; the censer covers the same ground with more depth |

## Known rough edges

- Cloudstepping is triggered by the shape of your jump rather than by a keypress, because the mod
  deliberately avoids client-side packets. A keybind would feel better and is a small addition.
- The Echo Mirror shows echoes as particles and text lines rather than as animated figures, since
  figures would need models.
- Formation surveying re-reads the circuit every three seconds rather than reacting instantly to a
  single tile being placed.
